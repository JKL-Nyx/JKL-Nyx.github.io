03 — Selected work

What I built this year

Six projects from first year. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

Amberlight

Unreal Engine 5 · Solo · 8 weeks

A 3d platformer game made using blueprints in Unreal Engine, the player must avoid turrets and hazards as they try to escape their prison.

My role: Everything - Gameplay , Spawning, AI, HUD and the end-of-round state.

Saving Santa

Unreal Engine 5 · Solo · 3 weeks

A 2d christmas themed action platformer written using blueprints in Unreal Engine, the player must fight their way through evil creatures as they try to save Santa.

My role: Everything - Gameplay, AI, Models, Environment, HUD.

Draugr

Unreal Engine 5 · Programming · 2 person team · 5 months

A 3rd person action game written using blueprints in Unreal Engine, the player must fight their way through waves of enemies on their journey of redemption.

My role: Gameplay, Programming, AI, HUD.

04 — About

About me

I am a first-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I came to the course from playing games and taking them apart, and I am here to learn to build them properly.

I am most interested in gameplay programming — the systems that decide how something feels to control. This year I have worked with Blueprints, modelled and textured my own assets, and recorded and implemented my own audio.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project.

Tools

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Blueprints
  • Blender
  • Substance Painter
  • Reaper and Audacity
  • Git and GitHub

Strengths

  • Gameplay programming
  • Level blockout and pacing
  • Sound design and implementation
  • Finishing things on a deadline
